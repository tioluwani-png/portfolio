    Where the numbers come from

    Every price is the national average retail price published by the National Bureau of Statistics in its monthly Selected Food Price Watch, fuel price watches (petrol, diesel, kerosene, cooking gas) and Transport Fare Watch. Prices in your market may be higher or lower.

    Food is priced per kg unless the item says otherwise. Data runs from . Some items only appear from January 2025, when NBS changed its list. The salary tool assumes 22 working days a month and 8 hours a day, and your pay never leaves your phone.
